# 2025 Quebec Local Return Coverage

This matrix describes the implemented local preparation/calculation workflow. It is not a certification statement and it is not a filing-submission capability.

## Supported return profile

The local calculator currently supports a bounded 2025 return when all of these are true:

- Full-year Canadian resident and full-year Quebec resident.
- Province on December 31 is Quebec.
- Single filing status with zero dependants.
- Employment calculations for ages 19 to 64.
- Quebec employment with supported T4 and RL-1 boxes.
- Covered student, tuition, scholarship, RESP educational-assistance-payment, RRSP, moving-expense, tips/other-employment-income, drug-insurance, instalment, Canada workers benefit, Quebec work-premium, Schedule B, additional T1/TP-1 filing-screen, and limited solidarity-credit facts are explicitly answered.
- Every required slip and account-review question is confirmed by the user.

Age 18 is blocked until QPP prorating is implemented.

## Supported slips and boxes

| Slip | Supported boxes and fields |
| --- | --- |
| T4 | Boxes 14, 17, 17A, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 44, 52, 55; province of employment; box 28 CPP/QPP, EI, and PPIP exemption indicators |
| T4A | Boxes 040, 042, 105, and 22. Box 042 is supported for RESP educational assistance payments. Box 105 is supported for ordinary post-secondary scholarships, bursaries, and awards. Box 040 is recognized only to block unsupported accumulated-income-payment cases. |
| RL-1 | Boxes A, B.A, B.B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, O, 211; optional box O allocations RB, RZ-RB, RU, and RZ-RU |
| T5 | Box 13 interest only |
| RL-3 | Box D interest only |
| T2202 | Box 24 total part-time months, box 25 total full-time months, box 26 eligible tuition fees |
| RRSP receipt | Amount and contribution period, either March-December 2025 or first 60 days of 2026 |
| RC210 | Boxes 10 and 11 for advanced Canada workers benefit amounts |
| RL-19 | Boxes A and B for supported Quebec advance payments; boxes C, D, G, and H are recognized only to block unsupported credits when positive |

Identifier fields such as SINs are not collected. T5 box 22 is not collected.

## Required explicit confirmations

Calculation stays blocked until the user answers the coverage and evidence gates, including:

- Income, deduction, credit, CRA, and Revenu Quebec record reviews.
- Unsupported-situation checklist.
- Federal and Quebec tuition questions, including the Quebec institution-location question.
- RRSP contribution, prior-unused, period-total, deduction-limit, deduction-requested, and HBP/LLP questions when applicable.
- Federal and Quebec instalment review and payment totals.
- Schedule B living-alone eligibility.
- Additional T1/TP-1 filing screens: immigration or emigration in 2025, Quebec trust return, separate post-death return, and Quebec enterprise registration or annual registration fee.
- Qualifying government student-loan interest and elected federal/Quebec claim amounts, including zero.
- Scholarship receipts, award category, qualifying-student status, attendance, part-time program cost pools, and T4A/RL-1 reconciliation when awards are present.
- RESP EAP review, other-payment and QESI special-tax questions, payment amounts, and T4A/RL-1 reconciliation when payments are present.
- Quebec prescription drug insurance months and exemptions.
- Canada workers benefit and Quebec work-premium eligibility, including advance-payment slips and amounts.
- Solidarity-credit eligibility and housing facts when the credit is requested.

## Current exclusions

The local calculator blocks, rather than estimates, returns involving:

- Any tax year other than 2025 or a province other than Quebec.
- Part-year or non-resident returns.
- Filing statuses other than single, dependants, deceased returns, or bankruptcy returns.
- Self-employment, capital gains, rental income, foreign income or foreign tax, foreign property over $100,000, crypto transactions, pension or benefit income, or Indian Act exempt income.
- Disability or caregiver claims, employment expenses, medical expenses, donations, and childcare expenses.
- Immigration or emigration in 2025, Quebec trust returns, separate post-death returns, and Quebec enterprise registration or annual registration fees.
- Non-government or unconfirmed student-loan interest.
- Moving expenses.
- Tips or other employment income not already captured by supported T4/RL-1 employment-slip fields.
- Scholarship or award categories other than ordinary post-secondary awards.
- RESP payment types other than beneficiary educational assistance payments, QESI cumulative amounts over $3,600, and T4A box 040 accumulated-income-payment cases.
- QPP disability pension or CPT30 QPP/CPP election branches.
- Non-Quebec employment and unsupported slip boxes or RL-1 box O allocation codes.
- Alternative minimum tax cases above the current covered gross-income threshold.
- Quebec tax shield, adapted work premium, work-premium supplement, and unsupported RL-19 advance-payment boxes.
- Schedule D different-address cases and owner dwellings without the supported municipal-tax-bill branch.
- NETFILE, ReFILE, Revenu Quebec electronic submission, credentialed government access, and automatic PDF/OCR extraction.

The federal and Quebec return exports are review artifacts only. Filing remains a separate user action through certified or authorized channels.

## Maintainer update checklist

When updating the 2025 calculation boundary:

1. Update the typed models, gates, formulas, sources, local schema/sample, and local UI fields together.
2. Keep official source IDs and formula provenance on every emitted line or blocker.
3. Add or update boundary fixtures for supported complete returns and blocked returns; include at least one no-claim fixture where new fact groups are explicitly reviewed as empty.
4. Run the focused return/gate/API/UI checks before changing this coverage page, then regenerate the sample line-status matrix from the current engine output.
5. Do not mark a branch supported in the UI or docs until unknown inputs block, unsupported inputs block, and the complete fixture calculates without blockers.
