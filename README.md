# TaxAgent Canada

TaxAgent Canada is a local-first project for understanding Canadian personal tax questions, with special attention to Quebec-specific tax software and filing decisions. It is intended to help users reason from their own records and official tax concepts.

TaxAgent does not submit returns, request CRA or Revenu Quebec credentials, connect to government accounts, or claim filing certification.

## Privacy and limits

Use TaxAgent only with records you are comfortable storing locally or sending to the configured model endpoint. Advisory prompts and conversation facts may be sent to the configured model endpoint; review its destination before using personal data. Local files and exported artifacts may be plaintext depending on the branch and workflow. Do not enter government-account credentials into this project.

TaxAgent is not certified tax software and does not provide certified tax, legal, accounting, or financial advice. Users should verify important decisions against CRA, Revenu Quebec, and their own records before filing.
